Implement Hybrid Manager

We'll write a Node.js utility on the GCP VM that manages these processes, monitors their health, and reports the live URL back to your Next.js app.

Why this is the "Pro" way

  • Reliability: If one service is down, your app still works.
  • Best of both: You get your custom name whenever possible, and a stable random one when you can't.
  • Transparent: The user just sees a "Live Preview" button; they don't care how the tunnel works.
