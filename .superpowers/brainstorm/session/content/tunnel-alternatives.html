Exploring other "Named URL" options

The Industry Standard

  • × Static domain requires paid plan
  • ✓ Most stable and fastest
  • × Free plan has strict bandwidth limits

Serveo.net

SSH-only (No binary needed)

  • ✓ Request custom subdomains for free
  • ✓ Zero installation on VM (uses SSH)
  • × Often down due to abuse

Bore.pub

Modern & Fast (Rust)

  • ✓ Extremely lightweight
  • × Usually gives you a random port, not a subdomain
  • ✓ Great for raw TCP connections
★

My Verdict

If Localtunnel isn't right, Serveo.net is the closest free match for custom subdomains (user.serveo.net). However, Localtunnel is generally the community favorite for free "named" URLs.

Why custom subdomains are tricky for free

Most companies (like Ngrok) make "Static Domains" a paid feature because it's expensive to manage the DNS and prevent spam. For a free major project, Localtunnel or Cloudflare (even with random names) are your strongest options.
